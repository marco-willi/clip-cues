A cue score is the cosine of the image's normalized 768-d CLIP embedding against a named antonym direction, and the plotted quantity is the content-controlled change dc = c(synthetic) - c(real); because the scene is approximately held fixed, it isolates the real-to-synthetic movement rather than the content. Each bar is labelled with the end of the cue the synthetic image moved toward. Under every image is the canonical detector's own score for it - the same detector whose extremes appear in Fig 3.
