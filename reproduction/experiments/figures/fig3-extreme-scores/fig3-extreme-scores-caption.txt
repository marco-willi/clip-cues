Test-set images with the lowest and highest logits of the canonical CLIP detector, z = w.h + b, for CNNSpot (A) and SynthCLIC (B); 5 per pole. Because the bias is constant, ranking by z is ranking by projection onto the detector's single decision direction. Each panel gives the raw logit, the class, and the image's origin. **The direction is far more stable than these particular images:** across five seeds the decision direction agrees at Sigma-cosine 0.989 and its cue profile at rho 0.991, but the top-50 image sets agree at only 0.73 (SynthCLIC) and 0.66 (CNNSpot) - so the montage illustrates a stable direction rather than making a claim about these images. CNNSpot files each real photograph under the generator group it is paired into, so its real panels are annotated with the corpus the benchmark actually drew them from (LSUN, with the object subsplit) rather than the group name; the ProGAN and BigGAN reals were additionally centre-cropped and resized to 256x256 by the benchmark authors, so they are preprocessed crops rather than raw photographs.
